## Why Custody Sequencing Matters

Custody is not a technical decision made during build. The custody principle is set at **L0 — Strategy & Value Definition**, and the custody model is a **legal, regulatory, and operational decision made at L2 — Solution Architecture**. It locks every downstream workstream.

Get it wrong or late and the consequences compound:

- L3 — Build & Integration cannot start custody, key, and wallet work without a confirmed custody model
- Smart contract audit scope depends on custody architecture
- L5 — Readiness & Transition requires the key ceremony completed and custody infrastructure live and tested before production release
- The G-OPS Gate at L6 — Operational Execution cannot be signed off without the cold/hot ratio confirmed in live operations

**The custody decision is a blocker for the L2 Architecture Approval Gate.** It cannot be deferred.
