## Why Onboarding Architecture Is a Delivery Risk

Investor onboarding is consistently one of the top three program blockers in regulated tokenization delivery. Not because the technology is complex — but because the decision architecture is deferred.

The pattern:
- KYC vendor is selected at L4 or later
- EDD trigger logic is not designed until investor pipeline is live
- MLRO is appointed late — creating a compliance bottleneck at exactly the moment volume increases
- Travel Rule configuration is treated as a post-go-live task

**Result:** go-live date holds, but the first investor cannot complete onboarding for 3–6 weeks after launch.

Onboarding architecture must be designed at **L2 — Solution Architecture**, built at **L3 — Build & Integration**, and operational by the **L5 Production Release Approval Gate**. It is a critical path item, not a compliance add-on.
